# 原创图像生成记录

使用内置 image_gen，通过两次独立生成完成；没有使用参考网站素材、第三方品牌标志或客户照片。人物与产品均为生成式场景示意。

最终站点素材：`assets/neighborhood.webp`、`assets/neighborhood-mobile.webp`，来自 A 原图。B 原图仅保留于设计研究。

## A 新品街区

Use case: stylized-concept. Create an exquisite original brand illustration for Lianye, a Chinese innovative consumer product launch network. Asset: wide 3:2 scene illustration, no typography or UI. Art direction A 'The living launch neighborhood': a sophisticated architectural miniature photographed from elevated isometric perspective, a continuous gently curving coral-red ribbon pathway connects three distinct human-scale pavilions on warm ivory ground: a cobalt-blue creator recording studio with camera tripod, a buttery-yellow community clubhouse with people around table exploring small AI companion robot, a pale mint experience shop with terracotta awning and a person trying smart glasses. In foreground a large sculptural white friendly innovative robot with small dark face and two luminous soft-blue eyes sits on a circular coral podium. Tiny human figurines interact meaningfully with products. Leafy sculptural trees, simple furniture, curved terrazzo floors. Rich tactile matte ceramic, paper and painted wood, realistic soft daylight shadows, museum model craft, magazine-quality art direction, charming yet professional and architectural. Restrained palette ivory, vermilion, cobalt, butter yellow and sage. Spacious composition, clear silhouette, no floating cards, no text, no labels, no letters, no logos, no sci-fi neon, no abstract network spheres. Everything sits on the same continuous physical ground, generous empty cream margins. Express innovation entering real everyday scenes rather than technology as machinery. Render at highest detail.

## B 开放展场

Use case: stylized-concept. Create an exquisite original brand illustration for Lianye, a Chinese new product launch network. Asset: wide 3:2 scene without typography. Art direction B 'Open field exhibition': sculptural contemporary design exhibition in a sunlit airy pale peach architectural courtyard. Bold oversized orange circular doorway arch in background, burgundy floor stripe connects three exhibition plinths of varying height. Central white small AI companion robot displayed on terracotta pedestal; left a creator holding a camera talks with product designer; right a curator in cobalt trousers shows smart glasses to visitors seated at a beautiful yellow curved bench. Behind, a small professional community table with three people and laptops. Photographic miniature maquette, realistic tactile plaster and wood, immaculate editorial composition, sunlight with long sharp shadows. Elegant architecture, sophisticated gallery rather than cute toy city, olive tree, warm ivory, orange, oxblood, sky blue accents. No UI, no panels, no cards, no text, no logos, no brand names, no dashboard, no sci-fi. One coherent scene, objects grounded, large negative space at edges. Widescreen gallery-camera composition.
